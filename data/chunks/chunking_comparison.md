# Chunking comparison (S2-05)

Generated by `make ingest` from `data/cleaned/`; do not edit by hand.

- **structure**: whole blocks packed up to 1500 characters, never across a top-level section, closed at a heading from 500 characters; a chunk under 500 joins a neighbour in the same top-level section when both fit. Only a block over the limit is split (sentences, list items, table rows with the header repeated).
- **fixed**: the document text cut every 1500 characters with 200 characters of overlap, each cut moved back to whitespace.

Columns: *p50 / p95 / Max* are chunk sizes in characters (nearest rank). *In one top-level section*: chunks whose text all comes from one top-level section (for an SDS, one of its 16 sections). *Tables kept whole*: tables whose full text is inside one chunk; tables over 1500 characters cannot be. *Start lowercase*: chunks that begin with a lowercase letter, a sign of a cut mid-sentence. *Characters* include the overlap and repeated table headers.
